# Monetization, and where the line is

## Models

- **Premium**: pay once. Simplest, most trusted; the design can serve fun alone.
- **Premium plus expansions**: big content packs later (new biomes, dimensions, bosses).
- **Free to play**: free to start, money from items.
  - *Durable* purchases last (a skin, a house style); *consumable* ones are spent (boosts, currency).
  - *Expression* items change looks; *enhancement* items change power.
- **Season pass**: a track of rewards earned by playing over weeks, with a free and a paid row.
- **Supporter or tip jar**: pay what you like, nothing locked.

## Good practice

- Sell expression, not power. Cosmetics, styles, extra save slots, creator tools.
- The free game must be a whole game, not a demo full of walls.
- Show real prices in real money; avoid layers of currencies that hide the cost.
- If anything is random, show the exact odds, and never sell random boxes to children.
- Put the shop where it makes sense (a tailor in the world, an item preview), not in pop-ups that
  interrupt play.
- Let players try or preview before buying.

## Dark patterns to avoid

- Pay-to-win: money buys advantage over others or skips the core challenge.
- Waits and timers built so the player pays to skip them.
- Fear of missing out: items that vanish forever, countdowns.
- Streaks or daily logins that punish missing a day.
- Loot boxes and gambling-like mechanics (also illegal or regulated in some countries).
- Confusing currencies, bundles priced so there is always some left over.
- Making the game worse to sell the fix (grind sold as a convenience).

## The tests

- Would you be comfortable explaining this to a child's parent?
- If the player knew exactly how it works, would they still feel good about paying?
- Does paying make the game more fun for the payer without making it less fun for anyone else?

## For a game made for one person or friends (like PixelRealm on Swift Playgrounds)

There is nothing to sell, and that is a strength: every design choice can serve fun. Use the same
ideas for motivation without money: cosmetic unlocks earned by play (suit paints, house styles,
titles in the journal), a season of goals in a mod, and sharing creations (mods, structures,
vehicles) as the long-term reason to play.
